Wade Lovell

Senior AI Strategy & Transformation Leader

Wade Lovell is principal of CognitionHive. His AI work began in a 1981 doctoral seminar in Econometrics, making him a founding-era practitioner in a field most people entered three decades later. Across that span he has served as CTO, Technical Architect, and AI Architect in banking, insurance, and enterprise software.

His doctoral dissertation at Walsh College focuses on LLM agent reliability in regulated settings. He is a published author on Ethical AI in Education, the Workforce, and the C-Suite. Author of three books: "Ethical AI in K-6 Education: Everyday Classroom Practice for Agency, Confidence, and Equity", "The Ethical AI Argument: Why Educators Must Rethink AI in K-6", and the forthcoming "AI or Extinction: The C-Suite Reckoning".

AI strategy and execution leader with 30+ years driving measurable business impact through large-scale digital transformation and technology adoption. Proven track record architecting and executing company-wide AI strategies, establishing governance frameworks, and mobilizing cross-functional teams across matrixed organizations. Deep expertise bridging technology, product, business strategy, and organizational capability to unlock AI-driven growth, productivity, and customer value.
