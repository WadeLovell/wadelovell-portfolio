## Frontier disclosure digest (what each report says about algorithm / reward source / async design / effort control)

- DeepSeek-R1 (Nature 2025): GRPO, rule accuracy+format rewards, RM avoided for hacking; V4 (2606.19348): per-domain SFT+GRPO specialists with domain reward models and length penalties, consolidated by full-vocab on-policy distillation; FP4 rollouts identical to deployment; DSec sandboxes with preemption-safe replay; think modes Non/High/Max (Max needs 384k context).

K3 (2607.24653): partial-rollout pausing at fraction lambda with sandbox-persisted resumption, per-token regularisation tolerating stale data, tau-scaled per-problem budgets (-1 reward over budget) annealed to produce low/high/max experts, agentic GRM with mandatory rubric protocol and verbosity cap, nine experts merged by multi-teacher OPD, co-located 1M-context RL with external CPU KV pool.
- GLM-5 (2602.15763): GRPO+IcePop (beta=2, no KL, eps 0.2/0.28, group 32, on-policy) for reasoning; fully async agent RL via Multi-Task Rollout Orchestrator, TITO gateway, Direct Double-sided IS clipping of rollout log-probs, staleness threshold tau on oldest rollout version, sandbox-failure filtering; deterministic torch.topk in DSA indexer; hybrid rule/ORM/GRM rewards; on-policy cross-stage distillation. GLM-5.2 (blog 2026-06-16): critic-based PPO on single rollouts to train compacted sub-traces (CompactionRL 2607.05378), effort levels High/Max, parallel OPD merging 10+ experts. GLM-5.3 (blog 2026-08-14): post-training-only scaling, synthesized environments with synthesized verifiers, train/rollout logprob diff 1e-7, multi-teacher OPD with teacher switching.
- Qwen3 (2505.09388): rule-reward reasoning RL, thinking-mode fusion, budget via forced stop, general RL on 20+ tasks; GSPO (2507.18071) later; Qwen3.5-Omni (2604.15804): specialists -> OPD -> interaction RL, DPO + GSPO.
- MiniMax M1 (2506.13585): CISPO, FP32 head; M2 series/M2.5/M2.7 (2605.26494, Forge blog): CISPO with reward-to-go, windowed-FIFO async, prefix-tree merging, process + completion-time rewards, single mixed-domain stage; M3 (blog 2026-05-31): adaptive thinking mode.
- OpenAI GPT-5 (2601.03267): RL on CoT, learned router, safe-completions objective, effort levels, parallel TTC; o3/o4-mini card: deliberative alignment (2412.16339 baseline), CoT monitoring; no algorithm disclosed. ## Negative or null results (evidence for the gap analysis)

- [2510.13786] Loss aggregation, curriculum, length penalty and advantage normalisation change compute efficiency but not the asymptote (leave-one-out at 16k GPU-h).
- [2505.09388] Qwen3: on an 8B student, RL gives no pass@64 improvement and distillation wins with 1/10 GPU-hours.
- [2504.13837 / 2507.14843] RLVR narrows solution coverage at large k; support shrinkage 2:1-3.6:1 even for ProRL checkpoints (AIME24 base pass@8192 93.3 vs ProRL 83.3).
- [2506.10947] Random/incorrect rewards give +21-24 MATH-500 on Qwen2.5-Math but nothing on Llama3/OLMo2; effect vanishes when clipping is removed.
- [2503.20783] GRPO length growth partly an optimisation bias (1/|o| and std terms), not emergent reasoning.
- [2505.15134] Entropy minimisation fails on Llama-3.1-8B math and on value-reasoning tasks.
- [2505.22617] Entropy bonus and reference-KL regularisation either are hyper-parameter sensitive or degrade performance.
- [2510.18874] KL regularisation and advantage estimation do not explain RL's robustness to forgetting; self-SFT on initial-policy samples still forgets.
- [tml:on-policy-distillation] SFT on a model's own KL-zero samples at any practical LR still degrades IF-eval (finite-batch drift).
- [2507.18071 / 2506.13585] GRPO diverges on MoE without routing replay; DAPO clip-higher insufficient at 16 off-policy updates per batch (MiniMax-M1).
- [2602.15763] Non-deterministic CUDA/TileLang top-k in the DSA indexer collapsed RL within a few steps with a sharp entropy drop; torch.topk fixed it.
- [2607.24653] Fine-grained top-k OPD objectives gave no advantage over full-distribution OPD in K3.
- [2602.02276] Rigid token budgets cause 'length overfitting' - models cannot exploit more inference compute (motivates Toggle).
- [2606.18521 / 2601.13572] Task-arithmetic/TIES/DARE merging degrades RL-trained models; RL task vectors are sparse and near-orthogonal.
- [openai:CoT_Monitoring_2025] Putting a CoT monitor in the reward yields obfuscated reward hacking; monitor recall drops to near zero.
- [2504.02495] Long-CoT DeepSeek-R1 scores below a 236B RFT reward model on RM benchmarks: reasoning length does not help generalist RM.
- [2508.05004] R-Zero collapses after ~3 co-evolution iterations; single-model (unified) variant collapses after 1.
- [2512.01848] SafeChain and R2D-R1 SFT give marginal or negative safety gains and reduce AIME/GPQA; only STAR-1 helps (SFT-dependent).
