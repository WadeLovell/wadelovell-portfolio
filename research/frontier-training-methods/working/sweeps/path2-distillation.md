## Frontier disclosure check (Kimi K3, GLM-5.2, Kimi K2.5)
- Kimi K3 (2607.24653) DISCLOSES distillation: Sec. 4.1.3 'We adopt Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation (MOPD) to consolidate these domain-specialized capabilities across varying reasoning efforts into a unified model [75, 135, 29]'; nine (domain, effort) teachers; per-token reward clip(sg(log pi_teacher/pi_theta), -Rmax, Rmax); 'we also experimented with more fine-grained top-k distillation objectives, we observed no clear advantage'. Refs: [75] Lu & Thinking Machines 2025, [135] MiMo-V2-Flash 2601.02780, [29] DeepSeek-V4 2606.19348.
- GLM-5.2 (z.ai/blog/glm-5.2, 2026-06-16) DISCLOSES distillation: 'we used the slime framework to conduct parallel OPD training, efficiently merging more than ten expert models into the final model. The entire OPD training process took approximately two days.' There is no separate GLM-5.2 report; GLM-5 (2602.15763) Sec. 3.5 describes On-Policy Cross-Stage Distillation with teacher checkpoints from earlier stages and GRPO group size 1.
- Kimi K2.5 (2602.02276) does NOT use the word distillation anywhere in the report; SFT data is 'synthesizing high-quality candidate responses from K2, K2 Thinking and a suite of proprietary in-house expert models'.

## Open problems
- [2604.00626] Distillation scaling laws for OPD: 'no equivalent framework exists for on-policy distillation, where the generation cost of student rollouts introduces an additional compute axis'; key question 'whether more on-policy data can compensate for a weaker teacher, or whether teacher quality sets a hard ceiling regardless of rollout volume'.
- [2604.00626] Agent-level distillation: 'the systematic degradation of teacher supervision with trajectory depth ... remains a challenging open problem'; uncertainty-aware feedback ('teachers currently supply point-estimate probabilities') is listed as a second foundational gap.
- [2604.13016] 'OPD's apparent free lunch of dense token-level reward comes at a cost, raising the question of whether OPD can scale to long-horizon distillation'; future work proposes hybrids of dense token supervision on short segments with sparse outcome rewards for long horizons.
- [2608.11829] Whether OPD transfers new capability at all: at K=1024 'OPD-trained models do not exceed their pre-OPD base models', so capability expansion from a stronger teacher through OPD is untested at scale.
- [2606.30406 / 2607.17247] Cross-family distillation: MOPD with a Qwen3-235B teacher 'diverges catastrophically'; Distilled RL states plain OPD is 'largely restricting OPD to within-family distillation'. No frontier report distils across model families.
- [2608.19098] 'open, rigorously reproducible recipes are conspicuously lacking' for multi-teacher OPD; standard M-OPD recovers only 35.6% of oracle headroom.
- [2502.08606] Distillation 'can not produce lower model cross-entropies than supervised learning when both learning processes are given enough data or compute'; the compute threshold beyond which distillation stops paying is student-size dependent and has not been measured at >12.6B scale.
- [2602.15763] Infrastructure: 'Currently, we utilize the inference engine to fetch teachers' logits. In the future, we plan to migrate the inference backend to the training engine' (train/infer logit mismatch in OPD).
- [2607.24653] Kimi K3 found 'no clear advantage' for top-k distillation objectives over sampled-token OPD 'in our setting'; the objective choice at trillion-parameter scale is unresolved.
- [2604.16830] 'capability distillation does not imply calibrated confidence'; OPD under privileged context produces a 'Scaling Law of Miscalibration'.
- [2606.15007] Nemotron 3 Ultra needs a 'MOPD warmup' SFT to align student rollouts with teacher-supported distributions and runs two MOPD iterations; how many iterations of teacher co-evolution help, and the cost of retraining >10 teachers per iteration, are not analysed.
- [2605.23857] In pretraining distillation 'an overly strong teacher can overwhelm the student'; teacher-student compatibility rather than teacher strength governs gains, with no rule for choosing the compatible teacher at scale.

## Negative results
- [2607.24653] Kimi K3: fine-grained top-k OPD objectives gave 'no clear advantage in either convergence speed or final performance' over the clipped sampled-token log-ratio reward.
- [2606.30406] MOPD: distilling from a cross-family Qwen3-235B-A22B teacher degrades math accuracy under the policy-gradient loss and 'diverges catastrophically around step 18' under top-k; same-origin teachers are required.
- [2604.13016] A stronger teacher (R1-Distill-7B) 'fails to yield any improvement' where a weaker JustRL-1.5B teacher recovers >80% of the gap; same-family 1.5B and 7B teachers are 'distributionally indistinguishable from the student's perspective'.
- [2608.11829] OPD improves avg@K but never expands the pass@1024 boundary; more previously solvable problems become unsolvable than the reverse ('illusory distillation').
- [2607.05184] Privileged self-distillation degrades thinking models by up to 17% relative avg@16 on AIME24/25 and HMMT25.
- [2604.16830] Standard OPD (SDFT/SDPO) drives confidence to ~0.996 and worsens overconfidence across settings.
- [2502.12143] Students <=3B do not consistently benefit from long CoT or large teachers (Small Model Learnability Gap).
- [2502.18001] Stronger teachers do not always produce better students in CoT distillation.
- [2605.23857] Very-strong-to-weak pretraining distillation (8B -> 1.7B) underperforms a compatible 3.8B teacher.
- [2502.02671] Offline distillation on a fixed teacher dataset exhibits teacher hacking; only online data or prompt diversity prevents it.
- [2502.08606] Distillation never beats supervised pretraining at sufficient compute; if the teacher must be trained for a single student, supervised learning wins.
- [2606.22600 / 2605.13643] Teacher signal on later rollout positions is nearly useless (last 30% of tokens 'barely learns anything'); local teachability collapses in strong-to-weak OPD.
- [2608.19098] Vanilla multi-teacher OPD recovers only 35.6% of oracle ensemble headroom, with instruction following degrading.
- [2506.10910] Magistral contradicts DeepSeek-R1's small-model claim: pure RL on a 24B base matches distillation on AIME24, although RL on top of the distilled checkpoint is best.
- [2503.19786] Gemma 3 ablation: for short training horizons the smaller teacher is better; the trend reverses only for long horizons.
- [2607.13399 / 2603.25562] Length exploitation (truncation or padding) and unreliable teacher guidance on student prefixes are recurrent OPD failure modes.
- [2602.02276] Kimi K2.5 discloses no distillation stage (only SFT on data synthesised from K2/K2-Thinking/in-house experts), so the K2.5 recipe offers no evidence for or against OPD.
