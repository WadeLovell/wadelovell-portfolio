## Negative or null results (evidence for gap analysis)

- [2509.02046] Optimizer speedups over well-tuned AdamW shrink from 1.4x (0.1B) to ~1.1x (1.2B) with no downstream gain; many claimed 2x speedups reflect weak baselines; Muon is overtaken by Soap/Kron at >=8x Chinchilla.
- [2509.01440] Sophia diverges at small batch beyond the Chinchilla horizon even with lower LR; SF-AdamW, Lion and Signum scale poorly; vanilla Muon (no weight decay on 2D params) degrades with model size.
- [2512.05620] Under standard parameterization the Muon/SOAP/Shampoo advantage vanishes with width; muP alone fails the SOAP coordinate check without blocking or spectral normalization.
- [2507.20534] Vanilla Muon on a 53B/9B-active MoE drives attention logits above 1000 (instability), motivating QK-Clip.
- [2607.20548] Applying Muon to Mamba conv1D weights hurts convergence (fall back to AdamW); SOAP shows loss spikes at large batch without per-step QR / KL covariance.
- [2508.19228] A 7B MTP model underperforms NTP on standard NLP benchmarks (MTP gains do not extend beyond code).
- [2512.24880] Unconstrained hyper-connections show severe instability at scale (Amax magnitude ~3000) and extra memory traffic.
- [2505.14669] HALO and Jetfire become unstable when ported to FP4; LSS-INT4 diverges beyond ~50 tokens/param.
- [2509.25149] Removing any of stochastic rounding, Hadamard transforms, 2D weight scaling or the BF16 first/last blocks worsens the 12B/10T NVFP4 run.
- [2501.02423] Beyond a 'critical data size', adding training tokens increases loss in low-precision FP training.
- [2505.14302] W4A4 QAT quantization error rises with more training tokens and coarser groups; FC2 activation outliers are the bottleneck.
- [2502.08606] Distillation cannot beat supervised pretraining given enough compute; training a teacher for a single student is worse than supervised learning.
- [2509.14786] Standard data-constrained recipes (more epochs or parameters) eventually overfit; weight decay 0.1 is 30x too small.
- [2507.15857] Masked diffusion needs up to ~16x more compute than AR in the single-epoch regime; AR overfits at high epoch counts.
- [2508.10975] Naive LLM 'continuation' of web documents barely beats repeating data; high-quality seed data alone is insufficient.
- [2506.08007] Independent review (pith.science) notes no matched standard-NTP control on the same corpus; the continual-NTP baseline collapsed 51.2->10.7.
- [2501.11873] Micro-batch load-balancing loss suppresses expert specialization; switching to global-batch mid-training never fully recovers from-scratch performance.
- [2510.04800] Placing hybrid blocks only at the ends of the network (sandwich) causes large quality drops; hybrids without normalization are unstable.
- [2606.30634] The original PipeDream schedule degrades by >0.03 loss at 16 stages even with Muon+error feedback; only constant-delay 2BW scales.
- [2605.08933] Fully head-wise MuonSplit leads early but is overtaken by full-matrix Muon later in training.
- [2505.22757] Static MTP hurts small (1.3B-3B) models; curricula are needed.
