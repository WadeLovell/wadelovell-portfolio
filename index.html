The Problem

"Regulators are asking. Your controls were built for human code."

Agentic coding operates at sub-minute timescales, outside pull request workflows, and leaves no cryptographic trail documenting the reasoning behind changes.

Regulatory Exposure

OCC 2011-12, FDA 21 CFR Part 11, and SOC 2 CC7 controls were designed for human code. Examiners are questioning agentic governance.

No Decision Trail

When agents modify payment calculations or auth paths, there is no captured context or reasoning documentation.

No Cryptographic Integrity

Human commits are signed. Agentic commits typically lack verification mechanisms entirely.

Policy Enforcement Gap

Sensitivity rules enforced at merge for humans. Agents bypass these gates entirely and leave no record.
